- **ok**: minutes agree within 15% (at least 30 minutes), and SofaScore goals within 1 of FBref.
- **partial**: the player changed leagues and the provider only has one of them. Its numbers are real but cover fewer minutes.
- **suspect**: disagrees with FBref for a single club: most likely another player's record was merged in.
- **missing**: the provider has no record for the player.

## 6. Rules for the model

1. **Missing is never zero.** A missing value is excluded from a comparison, and the score shows reduced confidence. Exception: SofaScore CL xG/xA is empty when there is nothing to measure (no shots), which is a real zero.
2. **Per-90 uses the provider's own minutes.** Understat stats are divided by Understat minutes and SofaScore stats by SofaScore minutes, never by FBref minutes.
3. **Partial providers** (transfers between leagues) are used for per-90 rates only, and flagged. Season totals come from FBref, which covers every club.
4. **Suspect records are treated as missing.**
5. **Penalties:** FBref goals include penalties. Non-penalty goals = goals − SofaScore penalty goals, only when SofaScore is trusted for the player.
6. **League and CL xG come from different models** (Understat vs SofaScore). Compare each within its competition; never add them.
7. **League and CL stay separate** until the competition-context phase decides how to combine them; a CL goal is never counted in the league totals.
